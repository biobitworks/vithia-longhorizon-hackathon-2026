The problem 02

AI forgets how science got here.

Biology programs span papers, assays, omics, structures, model outputs, failed experiments, lab notes, and multiple AI sessions. The answer may survive. The scientific lineage often does not.

What evidence?

Which exact observations and sources produced the recommendation?

What changed?

Did new data change the bytes, the interpretation, or only the context?

Can we continue?

Can another scientist or agent recover the state without reconstructing it from chat?

The product 03

Persistent scientific state. Bounded model context.

evidence ↓ FCO atoms → typed FCG scientific state ↓ Vithia selects the minimum relevant context ↓ AI model / agent ↓ experiment or decision ↓ new observation ↓ immutable successor state

Vithia separates a growing evidence graph from the small context a model actually needs for the decision in front of it.

Biology wedge 04

Start where biological meaning changes faster than the underlying sequence.

Protein disorder, aggregation risk, target prioritization, and assay interpretation are natural first workflows: the same sequence can acquire a different experimental meaning as structure, disease context, or assay evidence changes.

sequence + structure + disorder + assay evidence ↓ Vithia scientific state ↓ AI proposes interpretation / experiment ↓ wet-lab result ↓ successor state — predecessor preserved

The pitch is for a verifiable research infrastructure layer; current public Vithia evidence is engineering validation, not a claim of wet-lab biological validation.
